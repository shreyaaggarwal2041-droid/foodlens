FOOD KNOWLEDGE

Look beyond the front of the pack.

Ingredients and nutrition labels can tell you much more than a product's marketing claims.

🍬

Added sugar

Sugar can appear under different names in ingredient lists. Check the ingredient list as well as the nutrition panel.

🌾

Fibre matters

Fibre is listed separately on nutrition labels and can help you compare products with similar calories.

🧂

Sodium is not just “salt”

Sodium can come from several ingredients. The nutrition panel gives you the amount per serving or per 100 g.

🥣

Serving size changes the story

Compare products using the same basis, such as per 100 g, before deciding which one has more of a nutrient.

MYTH BUSTING

Myth or fact?

Let's check
MYTH

“Low sugar” automatically means healthy.

Fact: One nutrient does not tell the whole story. FoodLens looks at multiple parts of the nutritional profile.

MYTH

“Natural” means the product is automatically better.

Fact: Marketing words should not replace checking the ingredient list and nutrition information.

MYTH

Shorter ingredient names always mean a healthier product.

Fact: Ingredients should be understood in context. The nutrition panel and overall product profile matter too.
